Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This page explains how we handle uploaded data.

  • No Storage: We do not store any images that you upload.
  • Temporary Processing: Uploaded files are processed in-memory and deleted immediately after scanning.
  • No Sharing: We do not sell or share your data with any third party.
  • Analytics: We only use Google Analytics to understand basic site usage, without identifying you personally.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us.
